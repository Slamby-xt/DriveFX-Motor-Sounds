Terms, Refunds & Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Who we are

DriveFX (drivefx.pro) is run by an individual sole trader based in the United Kingdom. For help with DriveFX, visit our Contact page. For purchases sold through Link, Link is the merchant of record through Stripe Managed Payments and handles checkout and order support.

What you get

DriveFX plays virtual engine sounds in your browser that follow your speed. Free: 5 cars and 5 minutes of engine time per device. Pro: every car and no time limit. DriveFX is entertainment; it is not a driving aid. Keep your eyes on the road and obey the law. Do not interact with the screen while driving.

Plans and prices

  • Monthly and Yearly are subscriptions. They renew automatically at the end of each period, and charge your payment method until you cancel.
  • Lifetime is a single payment with no renewals, for as long as DriveFX is offered. Upgrading to Lifetime on the same DriveFX ID stops renewal of your current subscription.
  • DriveFX sets the price currency from your network country. We use pounds sterling in the UK and Crown dependencies, euros in countries that use the euro, and US dollars elsewhere. If the device opening checkout is in a different pricing region, you can review its price before continuing. Stripe may also offer a local-currency conversion. Review the final currency and total, including any applicable tax, before you pay.

Cancelling a subscription

Open DriveFX and select your plan badge, then Manage subscription. You can also open your orders in Link with the email you used at checkout. Select your DriveFX order and cancel the subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps Pro active through the period you paid for. Contact Link support if you need help cancelling.

Refunds and your rights

To request a refund or exercise a legal right to cancel your purchase, contact Link support with your order details. You can also contact DriveFX for help. Your statutory rights apply, including if the product is faulty or differs from its description.

Your DriveFX ID

Pro belongs to the ID shown in the payment window (DFX-XXXX-XXXX), which is stored in your browser. The DriveFX confirmation page also displays it. Keep it: it restores Pro if you clear your browser data or use another browser. If you lose it, contact us from the address you paid with.

Privacy

  • Location: your GPS speed is read in your browser to drive the sound. It is not sent to us or stored.
  • Free trial counter: to stop the free minutes being reset by clearing cookies, our server stores the minutes used and keyed hashes derived from your DriveFX ID, device fingerprint and network (the first part of your IP address). Trial records expire after about 13 months without increased usage.
  • Currency: your country, taken from your IP address when you open the payment window, is used to set the price currency to USD, EUR or GBP.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe Managed Payments through Link, which sends receipts and invoices. We use purchase references and status to provide Pro and can look up a purchase by email for support. Stripe collects billing details at checkout. We do not receive your card number. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Visit statistics: we count visits with a cookie-free, anonymous counter (page, country, device type, browser and where you came from). No IP address is stored; a one-way daily hash lets us count unique visitors and cannot be used to follow you across days. If your browser sends Do Not Track, nothing is counted. The statistics are kept in an Upstash Redis database.
  • Hosting: Vercel serves the site and its license API. Neon hosts the license and trial database.
  • You can contact us to ask for access to or deletion of your data. Payment records may need to be kept for tax and accounting.
