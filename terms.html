Who we are

What you get

DriveFX plays virtual engine sounds in your browser that follow your speed. Free: 5 cars and 5 minutes of engine time per device. Pro: every car and no time limit. DriveFX is entertainment; it is not a driving aid. Keep your eyes on the road and obey the law. Do not interact with the screen while driving.

Plans and prices

  • Monthly and Yearly are subscriptions. They renew automatically at the end of each period, and the card you paid with is charged, until you cancel.
  • Lifetime is a single payment with no renewals, for as long as DriveFX is offered.
  • Prices are shown in US dollars, euros or pounds sterling, and what you see at checkout is what you pay. If a tax applies to your purchase it is shown before you pay.

Cancelling

Open DriveFX, tap your plan badge in the top bar, then Manage subscription. Sign in through Link or the Stripe billing portal with the email you used to pay. You can cancel, update your payment method or view invoices there. Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

This does not affect your legal rights if something is faulty or not as described. If anything goes wrong, or you were charged by mistake, email us and we will put it right.

Privacy

  • Location: your GPS speed is read in your browser to drive the sound. It is not sent to us or stored.
  • Free trial counter: to stop the free minutes being reset by clearing cookies, our server stores the minutes used and keyed hashes derived from your DriveFX ID, device fingerprint and network (the first part of your IP address). Trial records expire after about 13 months without increased usage.
  • Currency: your country, taken from your IP address when you open the payment window, is used only to suggest USD, EUR or GBP.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe Managed Payments through Link, which sends receipts and invoices. We use purchase references and status to provide Pro and can look up a purchase by email for support. Stripe collects billing details at checkout. We do not receive your card number. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Hosting: Vercel serves the site and its license API. Neon hosts the license and trial database.
  • You can ask us to access or delete the data we hold about you by emailing us. Payment records may need to be kept for tax and accounting.
